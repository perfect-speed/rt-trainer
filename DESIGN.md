# v0.12.2 design note

## Kalmar geometry
The training graphic is based on ESMQ AIP geometry, not an invented generic airport. The main runway is RWY 16/34 (2050 x 45 m). RWY 05/23 (656 x 40 m) crosses the southern part. AIP lists Apron 1, Apron 2, TWY A, A1, A8 and A10; TWY A connects the apron system and runway access. For the RWY 16 departure exercise, the schematic places the aircraft on the apron, taxis it north on the parallel taxiway, stops it at a holding point before RWY 16, then moves it onto the runway only after line-up clearance.

This is deliberately an approximate pedagogical map. It must never be presented as an operational aerodrome chart.

## Callsign state
Full registration is required at initial contact. Once ATC has established communication and introduces `S-QX`, both `S-QX` and the spoken Swedish spelling `Sigurd Qvintus Xerxes` are accepted from the learner.

## Speech
The v0.9.2 OpenAI + VHF DSP baseline is retained. Normal digit pronunciation is used for good communications. Clarified forms (nolla/tvåa/trea/femma/sexa/nia) remain a future degraded-radio training variable.
